Help name the Herdr client

The native window for the Herdr daemon is called Herdr UI for now, and it is getting a real name. Your agents are the flock, so the names come from the dogs that keep one and the work they do. An X poll holds four options, so the names are split into four polls by theme; the winner of each goes to a final. The icons get a poll of their own once the name is settled.

Names Polls open now

Wherever the app needs to say what it works with, it would read as “Shep for Herdr”, “Drover for Herdr”, and so on.

Icons Poll comes next

Each sketch is shown large, then at 48, 32 and 16 px, the sizes it shrinks to in the Dock, menus and Finder lists. Any of them can go with any name. There are thirteen, so they'll go to four polls of three or four, then a final.

The icons are early sketches; the winner gets a proper design pass.

Whatever the name, the app stays an independent client. Herdr itself, its daemon and its terminal UI, are a separate project at herdr.dev.
