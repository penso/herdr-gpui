Herdr UI is now Sheprd.

Same app, new name, and a new home at sheprd.org.

  • Your install keeps working. The in-app updater follows the rename, and your settings stay where they are.
  • Your sessions are untouched. Terminals and agents keep running through the rename.

Sheprd is an independent, open-source (Apache-2.0) client for Herdr's terminal servers. It is not made, endorsed or supported by the Herdr project.
