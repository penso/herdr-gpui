TestFlight beta iPhone and iPad · iOS 26

Your agents, in your pocket.

See which agent needs you, answer it, read its chat and open its terminal from your phone. The app pairs with Sheprd on your computer; nothing runs on the phone itself.

Join the TestFlight beta

On a computer? Open sheprd.org/testflight on your iPhone or iPad.

  1. 1

    Get TestFlight

    Install Apple's free TestFlight app from the App Store on your iPhone or iPad.

  2. 2

    Join the beta

    Tap Join the TestFlight beta on the device, then Accept and Install in TestFlight. New builds arrive there as updates.

  3. 3

    Pair your computer

    In Sheprd on your computer, turn on remote control in Settings and open pairing. Scan the QR code with the app, or keep the phone nearby to pair over Bluetooth.

Beta builds can change or break between updates, and the invite link can change between betas: this page always has the current one. Found a problem? Open an issue.
